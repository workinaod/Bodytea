# R6: Safety Boundaries & Functional Constraints Evidence Pack

Research job R6 for BodyT. Deterministic coaching PWA, no runtime LLM, general wellness, never diagnoses.
Consumers of this pack: onboarding gate (classify free text + answers), plan generator (must be unable to bypass RED), adaptation engine (day-of symptoms).
Access date for all sources: 2026-08-18. Researcher: Claude (session d21c12d6). No production code in this pack.

Conventions used throughout:
- **Tier**: GREEN (adapt normally), YELLOW (constrain + ask 1-2 questions), RED (stop automatic programming, no substitute workout, suggest professional care).
- **confidence: source** = grounded in a tier-A document captured below. **confidence: house** = HOUSE HEURISTIC, evidence-informed but not from a tier-A screening source. Never presented to users as medical guidance either way.
- No em dashes appear in any `userCopy` string. Copy is suggest-only, kind, non-alarmist, and never names a diagnosis.

## 3. GREEN / YELLOW / RED DECISION RULES (ACSM logic + PAR-Q+ overlay)

### 3.1 Inputs

Per ACSM (RIEBE-2015, ACSM-ALG) the decision uses exactly three axes plus intended intensity:
1. **Current activity**: "Performing planned, structured physical activity for at least 30 minutes at moderate intensity on at least 3 days/week for at least the past 3 months" = regular exerciser (ACSM-ALG, verbatim).
2. **Known disease**: cardiovascular (cardiac, peripheral vascular, or cerebrovascular), metabolic (type 1 or 2 diabetes), or renal disease (ACSM-ALG). ACSM 2015 deliberately dropped risk-factor counting (age, smoking, cholesterol, obesity, family history): "The new ACSM guidelines no longer include cardiovascular risk factor profiling as part of the exercise preparticipation algorithm" because the old approach flagged ~95% of adults and deterred participation (SCHWARTZ-2019).
3. **Signs or symptoms** suggestive of CV, metabolic, or renal disease, at rest or during activity (the RF rows above; ACSM-ALG verbatim list: pain or discomfort in the chest, neck, jaw, arms, or other areas that may result from ischemia; shortness of breath at rest or with mild exertion; dizziness or syncope; orthopnea or paroxysmal nocturnal dyspnea; ankle edema; palpitations or tachycardia; intermittent claudication; known heart murmur; unusual fatigue or shortness of breath with usual activities).
4. **Intended intensity**: light 30-39% HRR, 2-2.9 METs, RPE 9-11; moderate 40-59% HRR, 3-5.9 METs, RPE 12-13; vigorous 60%+ HRR, 6+ METs, RPE 14+ (ACSM-ALG). CDC talk test: moderate = can talk but not sing; vigorous = cannot say more than a few words without pausing for breath (CDC-INTENSITY). BodyT never surfaces %HRR; use the talk test in copy.

### 3.2 Core decision table (ACSM algorithm, branch by branch, ACSM-ALG verbatim recommendations in quotes)

| Current activity | Disease | Symptoms | ACSM recommendation | BodyT tier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Not regular | None | None | "Medical clearance not necessary. Light- to moderate-intensity exercise recommended. May gradually progress to vigorous-intensity exercise following ACSM Guidelines" | GREEN with ramp: start light-moderate, vigorous only after a gradual progression period (see 3.4) |
| Not regular | Known CV / metabolic / renal | None | "Medical clearance recommended. Following medical clearance, light- to moderate-intensity exercise recommended. May gradually progress as tolerated" | RED for automatic programming: pause, suggest clearance. After user confirms clearance: YELLOW (moderate cap + conservative dose) |
| Not regular | Any status | Any sign/symptom | "Medical clearance recommended" (symptom rows always dominate) | RED |
| Regular | None | None | "Medical clearance not necessary. Continue moderate- or vigorous-intensity exercise. May gradually progress following ACSM Guidelines" | GREEN, zero gating questions |
| Regular | Known CV / metabolic / renal | None | "Medical clearance for moderate-intensity exercise not necessary. Continue with moderate-intensity exercise. Medical clearance (within the past 12 months if no change in signs/symptoms) recommended before engaging in vigorous-intensity exercise" | YELLOW: continue moderate freely; vigorous blocked unless user confirms clearance within past 12 months and no symptom change |
| Regular | Any status | Any sign/symptom | "Discontinue exercise and seek medical clearance. May return to exercise following medical clearance. Gradually progress as tolerated" | RED |

The load-bearing ACSM principle, kept verbatim because the plan generator encodes it: **an asymptomatic person who already exercises regularly may continue moderate-intensity exercise even with known disease; clearance is only recommended before vigorous.** And symptoms trump everything: any sign or symptom means discontinue and seek clearance regardless of fitness or history.

### 3.3 PAR-Q+ overlay (where the two sources differ, both preserved)

PAR-Q+ is stricter than ACSM in scope: it screens conditions ACSM's algorithm ignores (respiratory disease, mental health conditions, spinal cord injury, stroke history, cancer, any chronic condition, medications). ACSM 2015 explicitly narrowed to CV/metabolic/renal. Disagreements and our house resolution:

| Topic | PAR-Q+ position | ACSM position | BodyT house resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| High blood pressure | Screened in Q1; follow-up 4b: resting BP at or above 160/90 (or unknown) routes to professional guidance | Hypertension alone is a risk factor, not "known CV disease"; algorithm does not gate on it | YELLOW on declared HBP: ask TQ-BP; at/above 160/90 or unknown-and-uncontrolled blocks vigorous and nudges a checkup; controlled below 160/90 = GREEN with vigorous ramp watch |
| Respiratory disease (asthma, COPD) | Screened; follow-up 7c flags current symptoms (chest tightness, wheezing, laboured breathing, cough more than 2 days/week, rescue inhaler more than twice in the last week) | Dropped from the 2015 algorithm | YELLOW on declared asthma/COPD: ask TQ-RESP; symptomatic per 7c = RED (pause + care suggestion); controlled = GREEN with warm-up note |
| Chronic non-cardiac conditions (e.g. old polio, epilepsy, arthritis) | Any YES routes to follow-ups; any follow-up YES routes to ePARmed-X+ or a qualified exercise professional | Not part of the algorithm at all | Follow ACSM for the cardiac gate; treat stable non-cardiac conditions as YELLOW-functional (constraints + optional professional nudge), not RED. Rationale: BodyT is suggest-only general wellness, and PAR-Q+'s own follow-up-all-NO path clears the person for gradual activity |
| Pregnancy | "Delay becoming more active... talk with your health care practitioner... and/or complete the ePARmed-X+ before becoming more physically active" | Not covered | YELLOW with ACOG/CDC constraints (Section 5) + provider-conversation nudge; RED only on ACOG warning signs or stated contraindication conditions |
| Age + vigorous | "If you are over the age of 45 yr and NOT accustomed to regular vigorous to maximal effort exercise, consult a qualified exercise professional before engaging in this intensity of exercise" | No age term in the algorithm | Over 45 + not vigorous-accustomed: vigorous progression gets the professional-consult nudge in copy but is not blocked (PAR-Q+ says consult, not stop). Marked in plan as a soft cap |
| Validity window | Clearance valid a maximum of 12 months and "becomes invalid if my condition changes" | Clearance within past 12 months acceptable for the vigorous branch if no symptom change | Adopt 12 months as the re-screen horizon for both; any new symptom invalidates immediately (both sources agree on that) |

### 3.4 What each tier means operationally

**GREEN** (adapt normally):
- No gating questions. Plan generator and adaptation engine run normally.
- New-to-exercise GREEN users still ramp: begin light-to-moderate; PAR-Q+ page 4 starting dose for people who needed follow-ups is the most conservative sourced dose we have and doubles as our universal floor: "20 to 60 minutes of low to moderate intensity exercise, 3-5 days per week including aerobic and muscle strengthening exercises", building toward 150 min/wk moderate (PARQ-2025; CDC-ADULTS). "Start slowly and build up gradually" (PARQ-2025). Specific week-over-week increment percentages are not given by any tier-A source captured here; any numeric ramp cap BodyT uses is HOUSE HEURISTIC and should say so in engineering notes, not in user copy.

**YELLOW** (constrain, then adapt):
- Exact constraints, each mapped to its source:
  - **Intensity cap at moderate**: sessions programmed to talk-test moderate (can talk, not sing; RPE 12-13 equivalent; 3-5.9 METs). Vigorous work (intervals, AMRAPs to failure, RPE 14+) is withheld (ACSM-ALG vigorous-clearance branch; CDC-INTENSITY definitions).
  - **Vigorous unlock condition**: user confirms professional clearance within the past 12 months with no change in symptoms (ACSM-ALG, verbatim "within the past 12 months if no change in signs/symptoms").
  - **Conservative dose floor**: start at the PAR-Q+ 20-60 min low-to-moderate, 3-5 d/wk envelope; progress gradually toward 150 min/wk (PARQ-2025).
  - **Ramp cap**: progression steps smaller and slower than GREEN. Magnitude is HOUSE HEURISTIC (no tier-A number exists); direction ("start low and progress slow") is sourced (SCHWARTZ-2019 quoting ACSM).
  - **1-2 targeted questions** from Section 4, chosen by which YELLOW fired. Never more than 2 before the first plan; remaining uncertainty resolves conservative.
  - Functional YELLOWs additionally apply the Section 5 movement constraints.
- YELLOW copy is invitational, never alarmed. Example (HBP): "We will keep sessions at a pace where you can talk but not sing while you and your doctor keep an eye on your blood pressure. Two quick questions so I can set this up well."

**RED** (stop automatic programming):
- Plan generator refuses to construct a plan; adaptation engine cancels pending suggestions; no substitute workout of any kind.
- App shows the matching RF userCopy, suggests professional care (emergency services when the phrasing is active-now for RF-CHEST, RF-NEURO, RF-HEAT).
- Exit path: an explicit user action "a professional cleared me" (with date), which drops the case to YELLOW (post-clearance branch: light-to-moderate, gradual progression per ACSM-ALG), never straight to GREEN. Symptom recurrence re-fires RED.

### 3.5 Day-of rules for the adaptation engine (FatigueNote, 14-day window)

- Any RED trigger phrase in a day-of note: fire the RF flow. Today's session is not replaced with a lighter one; it is paused (sourced: ACSM "Discontinue exercise and seek medical clearance").
- Joint pain flag on a joint (no RED phrasing): apply that joint's Section 5 constraint to today and the 14-day window; if the same joint flags on 3 or more sessions in the window, or the user says it is getting worse, escalate to a durable Prefs.limitations suggestion plus the CDC arthritis-style nudge: some soreness when starting is normal, pain that persists or worsens is provider territory (CDC-ARTH). Threshold of 3 is HOUSE HEURISTIC.
- Pregnancy declared at any point: switch to Section 5 pregnancy constraints immediately; ACOG warning signs join the RED vocabulary for all future notes (ACOG-804).

## 4. TARGETED QUESTION SET (PAR-Q+ derived)

Ask at most 2 per YELLOW event, in the order listed. Every question is adapted from a PAR-Q+ 2025 follow-up (question numbers cited). "Flips RED" = stop programming and suggest care; "Confirms YELLOW" = keep constraints; "Clears GREEN" = constraint may be dropped or reduced.

## 7. INTEGRATION NOTES

### 7.1 Mapping onto existing memory

- **Prefs.limitations (durable)**: extend from joint-only flags to the Section 5 key set: 7 joints + cannot-kneel, cannot-get-to-floor, cannot-raise-arm-overhead, uses-cane-or-support, dizziness-on-standing, pregnancy-T1/T2/T3, postpartum-early. Pregnancy and postpartum entries MUST carry an asOf date and auto-advance/expire (pregnancy trimester advances by calendar; postpartum-early expires after the staging window or on user confirmation). A durable store that can silently keep "pregnancy-T2" forever is a correctness bug, not just staleness.
- **FatigueNote pain flags (14-day window)**: day-of YELLOW constraints only; they never write Prefs.limitations directly. Repetition rule (3+ flags on one joint in the window, or worsening language) produces a suggested durable limitation the user confirms. Suggest only, never auto.
- **Safety tier itself** is session-scoped state derived at onboarding and re-derived whenever: a new note matches any RF trigger, a limitation is added, pregnancy is declared, or the 12-month re-screen horizon passes (PARQ-2025 validity window). Persist the last screening date and the clearance-confirmed date (for the ACSM 12-month vigorous rule).

### 7.2 Onboarding gate

Pipeline order (deterministic, no LLM):
1. Normalize free text (lowercase, strip punctuation, stem).
2. **Negation guard**: suppress a trigger if a negator ("no", "not", "never", "without", "don't have") occurs within the preceding ~4 tokens. Case 19 is the regression test.
3. **RED pass** (Section 2 triggers). First match wins; multiple matches keep the highest-priority copy (active-emergency phrasing > care-soon phrasing). RED ends the pipeline: no plan object is constructible.
4. **YELLOW pass** (declared conditions, PAR-Q+ Q1-Q7 equivalents from the structured answers + free text). Select at most 2 targeted questions (Section 4) ranked by tier-flip potential.
5. **Functional pass** (Section 5 mappings) - runs even inside YELLOW; constraints compose (union of avoid-lists, min of impact caps).
6. Context check: cardiac-referral words (chest, jaw, neck, arm + exertion/pressure/breath words) are evaluated in step 3 before the joint mapper can claim "shoulder/arm" mentions.

The existing onboarding injuries question becomes the free-text inlet for steps 3-5; the structured PAR-Q+-style YES/NO answers (if/when added) feed step 4 directly.

### 7.3 Plan generator hard gate

Make RED unbypassable by type, not by runtime check: the generator's entry point takes a discriminated union where the RED variant carries no plan-constructing data (only userCopy + clearance callback). There is no code path from RED to a Workout. YELLOW is a constraints object the generator must consume (intensity ceiling, dose floor/ceiling, avoid-list, question queue). structure.test.ts layering: safety rule tables live beside plan (pure data + pure classify functions), consumed downward by engine/store; no upward imports.

### 7.4 Suggested typed shape (safetyRules.ts)

```ts
export type SourceRef =
  | 'PARQ-2025' | 'ACSM-ALG' | 'RIEBE-2015' | 'SCHWARTZ-2019'
  | 'CDC-ADULTS' | 'CDC-INTENSITY' | 'CDC-OLDER' | 'CDC-ARTH'
  | 'CDC-STROKE' | 'CDC-MI' | 'AHA-MI' | 'CDC-RHABDO' | 'CDC-HEAT'
  | 'ACOG-804' | 'CDC-PREG' | 'HOUSE';

export type ConstraintId =
  | 'shoulder' | 'elbow' | 'wrist' | 'lower-back' | 'hip' | 'knee' | 'ankle'
  | 'cannot-kneel' | 'cannot-get-to-floor' | 'cannot-raise-arm-overhead'
  | 'uses-cane-or-support' | 'dizziness-on-standing'
  | 'pregnancy-T1' | 'pregnancy-T2' | 'pregnancy-T3' | 'postpartum-early'
  | 'moderate-intensity-cap' | 'conservative-dose' | 'vigorous-needs-clearance';

export interface SafetyRule {
  id: string;                    // e.g. 'RF-CHEST', 'TQ-BP', 'FC-KNEE'
  tier: SafetyTier;              // tier this rule assigns when triggered
  triggers: string[];            // normalized phrase fragments; classifier stems + windows them
  negationGuards?: string[];     // extra rule-specific negators beyond the global list
  constraint: ConstraintId | null; // null for RED (RED constructs no plan)
  userCopy: string;              // kind, non-alarmist, suggest-only, NO em dashes
  sourceRef: SourceRef | SourceRef[];
  confidence: 'source' | 'house-heuristic';
  followUps?: string[];          // TQ ids to ask (max 2 consumed by the gate)

```

Tier precedence when multiple rules fire: RED > YELLOW > GREEN; within YELLOW, constraints union; `confidence` is carried through to the debug/owner view only, never shown to users.

### 7.5 Copy and posture rules (restating repo non-negotiables as they bind this feature)

- Suggest only, never auto: the app never contacts anyone, never books anything, never sends SMS. RED copy suggests care; the user acts.
- No em dashes in any userCopy string (all strings in this pack comply; keep a lint check).
- Never diagnose: copy names what the user reported ("chest pressure", "dark urine"), never a condition ("angina", "rhabdomyolysis" appears nowhere in user-facing text).
- Clearance is user-reported, date-stamped, and expires per the 12-month rule; new symptoms invalidate it immediately (PARQ-2025 + ACSM-ALG agree).
- Users never pick reps: constraints alter what the engine selects; they never open a menu.
